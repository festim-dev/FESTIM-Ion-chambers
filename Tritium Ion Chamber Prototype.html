Tritium Ion Chamber Prototype
FESTIM 2.2-rc.2 · drift terms · 2D axisymmetric

Coaxial ionisation chamber for tritium in air

A 1 litre chamber with a central anode at +500 V, filled with room air. Tritium betas ionise the gas, electrons attach to O₂ within microseconds, and positive and negative ions drift along the field lines to the electrodes, recombining on the way. The model predicts the chamber current against tritium concentration and the aspect ratio that keeps the response linear longest.

1 · Model

Three charged species drifting in a fixed Laplace field

Each species obeys a Nernst–Planck balance, J = −D∇c + c v, with v = −zμ∇φ supplied by FESTIM's ElectromigrationTerm. Electrodes absorb what the field drives into them through OutflowBC and inject nothing. The reading is the anode current computed from the Shockley–Ramo weighting field.

# species (m⁻³) e⁻ free electrons · P⁺ positive ions · N⁻ negative ions (O₂⁻)
∇·Je = S − νa ne − β nP ne μₑ = 0.04 m²/V/s, ν_a = 1e8 s⁻¹, β = 2e-13 m³/s
∇·JP = S − α nP nN − β nP ne μ₊ = 2.0e-4 m²/V/s
∇·JN = νa ne − α nP nN μ₋ = 2.2e-4 m²/V/s, α = 2e-12 m³/s
S(r,z) = A · (Ē/W) · fwall(r,z) Ē = 5.70 keV, W_air = 34.0 eV → 167.7 pairs/decay
∇·(r∇φ) = 0, φ = 500 V on rod, 0 V on can and end caps

Geometry. Anode rod of radius 1.5 mm on the axis, cylindrical cathode of radius R, end caps at the cathode potential, and 3 mm insulating rings where the rod passes through the caps. Reference shape: R = 5.0 cm, L = 12.7 cm (L/D = 1.27).

Source. Decays near a wall lose part of their energy into it. A straight-track Monte Carlo over the tritium beta spectrum with Katz–Penfold ranges in air (4.9 mm at the 18.6 keV endpoint) gives the local fraction kept in the gas, fwall, for each geometry, rod included.

Reading. I = e Σ qs ∫ Js·(−∇φ/V) dV. In steady state this equals the anode current exactly and avoids gradients evaluated on the electrodes. It agrees with the integrated anode flux to 0.01%.

Scope. The field is fixed (no space-charge feedback), but the distortion it would cause is computed afterwards from the charge density and reported. Still gas (no advection; flow speeds in a flow-through chamber are two to four orders below ion drift speeds), no columnar recombination along individual tracks, no tritium held on the walls, dry air at 20 °C and 1 atm.

2 · Fields

Ions follow the field lines; electrons never leave home

At 100 Ci/m³ in the reference chamber. Lines are electric field lines from the rod. Electrons attach to O₂ within about 10 ns, a few micrometres from where they are born, so their density simply mirrors the source. Positive ions pile up toward the can and negative ions toward the rod, and the two populations overlap most in the middle of the gap, where they recombine.

3 · Response

Reading versus tritium concentration

The current is linear over eight decades at 2.66e-20 A per Bq/m³, 1.0% below the ideal e·A·V·Ē/W because of wall losses. Ion–ion recombination bends the curve by 5% at 7.9 Ci/m³. The shaded region starts where the field from the ions' own space charge reaches 10% of the applied field (37 Ci/m³), computed by solving Poisson's equation on the converged densities. Beyond it the fixed-field result is indicative only.

4 · Aspect ratio

Same litre, different shapes

Holding the gas volume at 1 L and the bias at 500 V, the shape sets the trade-off. Flat chambers have a long drift gap and corners between the end caps where the field barely reaches; ions linger there, recombine, or diffuse to the cathode in pairs, so they lose signal even at trace levels. Long, thin chambers collect fast, which pushes recombination up by more than a decade in concentration, at the cost of slightly more beta energy lost to the can. Across the sweep the 5% nonlinearity point climbs from 0.2 Ci/m³ at L/D = 0.4 to 169 Ci/m³ at L/D = 7.0. Trace-level sensitivity peaks at L/D = 1.27, and the longest chamber gives up only 0.4% of it.

5 · Bias

Saturation curve of the reference chamber

Current against anode voltage for the L/D = 1.27 chamber, normalised to the fully collected current Isat. At 10 Ci/m³ the chamber reaches 99% collection at 1356 V; the 500 V design point gives 0.940. At 1 Ci/m³ 99% needs only 444 V, while at 100 Ci/m³ it needs more than 2000 V. Hollow points are where the ions' own space charge distorts the field by more than 10%, so the fixed-field result there is indicative only; it matters at low bias, where slow ions pile up.

The right panel is how a saturation curve is read in the lab. For volume recombination, 1/I is linear in 1/V² near saturation, so a straight-line fit over the high-voltage points and extrapolation to infinite bias gives Isat. Fitting the model points at 500 to 2000 V recovers Isat to 0.10%, a check that the model can stand in for a measured curve when sizing the bias supply.

6 · Checks

How far to trust the numbers

Charge conservation. The Ramo current and the integrated anode flux agree to better than 0.01%. At trace levels the extrapolated current matches the exact balance (generation minus recombination) to 0.09%.

Recombination. Boag's formula for a cylindrical chamber in continuous irradiation gives a collection efficiency of 0.779 at 100 Ci/m³ for the reference geometry; the model gives 0.704. The extra loss comes from the low-field corners near the end caps and the non-uniform source, which the 1D formula ignores.

Space charge. The perturbation potential is solved from −∇·(ε₀∇φ₁) = e(nP − nN − ne) with φ₁ = 0 on the electrodes, verified against the analytic uniform-charge coax solution to 1e-7. The reported distortion is ‖∇φ₁‖/‖∇φ‖ over the volume.

Numerics. FESTIM does not stabilise drift-dominated transport (ion cell Péclet numbers reach about 100 here), so each species gets a cell-local diffusion floor D = max(Dphys, μ|E|hE/2), with hE the cell length along the field. The floor vanishes where the field is weak, so it cannot fake losses in the dead corners. The drift velocity is kept at exactly −zμ∇φ by a four-line subclass of ElectromigrationTerm that uses the mobility instead of Einstein's relation. The remaining error is first order in h; each current is Richardson-extrapolated from Nr = 150 and 300 radial cells.

7 · Next

Where to take the prototype

  • Space chargeFeed the Poisson perturbation back into the potential the drift terms read and iterate to a fixed point. The solver is already in place for the validity check; coupling it extends the model past about 50 Ci/m³.
  • Metal wallA codimension-1 model of the stainless can: tritium uptake from the gas, diffusion and trapping in the wall, and release back into the chamber. This gives the memory effect and the slow background drift after an exposure, and the betas from wall-held tritium as an extra source term.
  • Bias and shapeRepeat the saturation curve for the long chambers and map the minimum bias for 99% collection against L/D and design concentration.
  • Flow-throughAdd gas advection through inlet and outlet ports with an AdvectionTerm, and the transient response to a concentration step.
  • Track effectsColumnar recombination along each beta track and electron scattering in the wall-loss Monte Carlo instead of straight tracks.
